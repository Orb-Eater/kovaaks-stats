Window — day-to-day scores are noisy (fatigue, warm-up, motivation); motor-learning research judges real skill consolidation over multi-week trends, not single sessions. 30 days (recommended) balances smoothing that noise against staying relevant to your current training. Compare vs — Previous window: this period vs an equal-length period right before it (most accurate, default). First half of window: the second half of this period vs its first half (reacts faster, noisier).

"All" uses first half vs second half of your history for comparisons — there's no earlier baseline for all-time.
