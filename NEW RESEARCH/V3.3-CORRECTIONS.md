## 1. STRIKE V3.2 §0.3 — resets are not a contamination source

> **V3.2 said:** "roughly a quarter of logged runs are abandoned attempts."

```
reset_runs  38 / 21775  (0.17%)
```

The 26.46% figure came from a broken elapsed-time proxy in the v1 validator that flagged
tracking scenarios for legitimately logging no kill rows. With the validated `Avg FPS`
detector the true rate is **0.17%**.

Resets do not meaningfully affect any statistic in this corpus. Keep the exclusion — it is
free and correct — but remove it from the build-order priority list.

### 1.1 New action: your app's restart counter is suspect

A session panel reported **12 restarts in one 41-run session** while only 38 reset files
exist across 22 months. That is roughly 30x more detections than artifacts.

Whatever heuristic the live build uses is firing on something other than resets. Replace it
with the `Avg FPS` rule from PARSER-SPEC §2 and re-check the session panel numbers.

`score_bias_if_kept -13.06%` is directionally correct but rests on very few scenarios
clearing the 5-reset threshold. Do not quote it as a corpus-level figure.

## 3. NEW — T12 is fragmentation, not noise

This replaces the V3.2 §4.1 framing, which attributed zero-measurable-scenarios to CV.

```
scenarios          2,118        (77 with 60+ runs)
distinct cm        90           top 8 cover 67% of runs
runs per scenario  ~10 mean
unit of analysis   scenario x cm-cluster
```

At `CM_CLUSTER_RATIO 1.1`, the 20–64 cm range alone spans ~13 clusters. Each well-played
scenario therefore fragments across several cm cells, and almost no cell accumulates the
~103 sessions per side that T4 requires.

**No estimator fixes this.** The cell space is larger than the run count can populate. The
statistics in V3 are correct; the corpus is spread too thin for them to resolve anything at
the scenario level.

### 3.1 What follows

1. **Benchmark aggregate as the headline is now the only viable primary metric**, not
   merely the best one. V3 §10a stands, strengthened.
2. **Report cell fragmentation explicitly.** A "your data is spread across N scenarios x M
   cm clusters" readout tells the user why everything says `within noise`, which is far
   better than the numbers simply never resolving.
3. **A tracked-scenarios list becomes essential rather than optional** (V3.2 §4.1 item 3).
   Committing to 2–3 scenarios at one cm is the only path to a measurable scenario-level
   number.
4. **Consider a coarser `CM_CLUSTER_RATIO` for this user.** 1.1 is defensible on
   perceptual grounds but produces 13 clusters over their working range. 1.25 would give
   ~7 and roughly double the runs per cell. This trades cm precision for measurability and
   should be a user-visible setting, not a silent default.
